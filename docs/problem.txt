1. About the Company
Dhatri Constructions is a real estate construction company in Hyderabad managing residential and commercial building projects. Site supervisors handle
daily operational expenses — buying tools, paying daily-wage labour, fuel, transportation, and small material items — using petty cash given to them
each morning. Without a structured tracking system, petty cash reconciliation is done at month-end from a bundle of paper receipts, making it impossible
to control costs at the project level in real time.
2. The Problem They Face
Dhatri Constructions gives petty cash to site supervisors daily across multiple active construction sites. All expenses are tracked on paper receipt
bundles submitted at week-end. Project managers do not know how much has been spent this week on each site until the receipts are submitted,
counted, and tallied manually.
Problem: Tracks petty cash issued to each construction site supervisor daily, records individual expense entries with category
(labour/material/tool hire/transport/food/misc), captures receipt photos, calculates running balance (issued − spent), and generates
site-wise weekly expense summaries for project cost control. At present, this type of work can become slow or scattered when it
depends only on calls, WhatsApp messages, spreadsheets, or manual follow-up.
This results in:
• Petty cash issued per site per day is tracked on paper — the total issued vs total spent for each site is unknown until the week-end reconciliation
• Expense entries are collected as paper receipts — some receipts are lost before submission, creating unverifiable cash gaps
• Project managers cannot see site-level spending by category (labour vs materials vs tools) — they cannot identify where cost overruns are
occurring
• Petty cash advances to supervisors are not linked to specific project budgets — overspending on one project is only discovered at month-end
• At month-end, finance spends 2-3 days manually categorising hundreds of paper receipts — this data arrives too late to control costs
3. What We Are Building
We are building the Construction Site Petty Cash & Daily Expense Tracker for Dhatri Constructions — a system where site supervisors log every
expense on their phone and project managers see site-level spending in real time.
How the system works:
• Step 1 - Admin registers each construction site with project name, site code, project manager, and monthly petty cash budget
• Step 2 - Each morning, admin issues petty cash to the site supervisor: site, supervisor name, amount issued, date
• Step 3 - Throughout the day, the supervisor logs each expense: category (Labour/Material/Tools/Transport/Food/Misc), amount, description, and
optionally a receipt photo
• Step 4 - Running balance is calculated: balance = total_issued_today − total_spent_today
• Step 5 - If balance goes negative, the system flags it as a cash shortfall requiring explanation
• Step 6 - Project manager views the site dashboard: today's issued, today's spent, balance, and category-wise breakdown
• Step 7 - Weekly expense summary shows: total issued, total spent, surplus/deficit, and category-wise total for each site
